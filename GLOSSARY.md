# Haunt Memo

A personal curated archive of ghost stories from YouTube, hand-picked by a single curator. Most Stories come from The Ghost Radio, but a Story can come from any YouTube Channel. It replaces an Instagram Story Highlight, which got too hard for fans to browse once it held around 100 clips.

## Language

**Archive**:
The whole collection of Stories the Curator has picked. Fans can see all of it.
_Avoid_: Library, playlist, highlight

**Story**:
A single YouTube clip that the Curator picked for the Archive. It can come from any Channel. One Story is one YouTube video.
_Avoid_: Clip, episode, video, post

**Channel**:
The YouTube channel that published a Story's clip, such as The Ghost Radio. The Archive isn't tied to one Channel.
_Avoid_: Show, program, source

**Curator**:
The single owner of the Archive. The Curator is the only person who adds, edits, or publishes Stories.
_Avoid_: Admin, owner, editor

**Synopsis**:
A short teaser of a Story (3–5 sentences) that sets up the tale without spoiling the ending. Gemini drafts it, and the Curator reviews it.
_Avoid_: Summary, description, note, review

**Display Title**:
A short, easy-to-read Thai title for a Story. It's shown instead of the original YouTube clip title.
_Avoid_: Name, headline

**Fan**:
An anonymous visitor who browses, searches, and listens to Stories. Fans have no account and can't change the Archive.
_Avoid_: User, member, visitor

**Storyteller**:
The person who tells the ghost story in a Story (usually the caller on the show).
_Avoid_: Caller, guest, narrator

**Tag**:
A category label for the kind of story, such as house ghosts, hospitals, or the military. A Story can have more than one Tag.
_Avoid_: Category, genre, label

**Scare Level**:
The Curator's own rating of how frightening a Story is, from 1 to 5.
_Avoid_: Rating, score, horror level

**Province**:
The Thai province where the events in a Story happened. Fans can filter by it.
_Avoid_: Location, region

**Place**:
An optional free-text description of the exact place where the events in a Story happened, such as a specific hospital or village.
_Avoid_: Location, venue

**Broadcast Date**:
The date the Story's YouTube clip was published on its Channel.
_Avoid_: Air date, upload date

**Added Date**:
The date the Curator added a Story to the Archive. Stories are listed by it by default.
_Avoid_: Created date

### Lifecycle

**Draft**:
A Story the Curator has saved but Fans can't see yet.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, hidden

**Published**:
A Story that Fans can see in the Archive.
_Avoid_: Live, public, visible
